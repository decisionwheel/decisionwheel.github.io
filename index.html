    Pro Tips

    Get the Most Out of Your Wheel

    1

    Use Equal Options for True Fairness

    Each segment gets equal probability. For weighted decisions, add an option multiple times to increase its chances — e.g. add "Pizza" three times to make it three times more likely.

    2

    Bookmark Your Custom Wheel

    After adding your options, use the Share button to generate a URL. Bookmark it for instant access to your recurring decision wheel — meeting roulette, lunch picker, and more.

    3

    Try Elimination Mode for Tournaments

    In Elimination Mode, each spin removes the winner. Run through a bracket of names or options without any repeats — ideal for competitions and contest draws.

    4

    Project on Screen for Group Events

    Use fullscreen mode and project the wheel onto a big screen at parties or in the classroom. The dramatic spin animation gets everyone excited for the result.
